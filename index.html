About this page

Covers only the bot's own trades (orders it placed) on Kalshi exchange shard 0, since it went live. Open positions are valued at the market midpoint (or the last trade when the book is thin), so open P&L moves until they settle. Account value = cash + positions. Deposits are not counted as profit. In each strategy row the outlined box is its profit (settled plus open at today's price); the figure beside it is profit per contract after fees, against the strategy's research par. The tick strip shows settled results oldest to newest: tall green ticks won, short red ticks lost. When two strategies hold the same market, each keeps its own row and P&L. Short codes (e.g. LKOL) come from the market ticker and match an open position to its settled row. The page updates itself every few seconds without reloading, and never places or cancels orders. Not affiliated with Kalshi.
